## Explain these six answers without notes

1. Who pays? Bounded software callable by an AI agent.
2. What does it buy? One newly requested contact observation.
3. Who receives the payment? The device operator's wallet.
4. What signs the answer? The ESP32's provisioned P-256 key.
5. What does the buyer verify? Source, request, signature, sample agreement, and age.
6. What remains unproven? Buyer demand, truthful sensing, and live peaq integration.
