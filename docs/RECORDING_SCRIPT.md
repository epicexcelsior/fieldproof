# Record the current FieldProof videos

[The current video scripts](competition/VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md) own the pitch, product demo, short feedback clip, and spoken copy.
[The physical rehearsal](competition/REHEARSAL.md) owns setup and recovery.

## Record screen and hardware together

Keep the powered board, your hand, and the relevant screen result visible.
Use a second camera or record the laptop screen separately. Clap once to align separate recordings.
The Seeker can serve as verifier or camera in separate takes.
The phone cannot film its own verifier screen at the same time.

Label BOOT as the physical test contact. Record a new job with an unchanged original receipt.
Record expiry and tamper rejection afterward. Keep test-token and pending-peaq labels readable.

## Finish one take

Preserve original files. Trim pauses without changing the apparent payment and measurement order.
Export a two-to-three-minute presentation and a separate product demo of at most three minutes.
Verify both videos without login before using their links in a form.
