## Working transaction

The laptop prepays 0.001 Devnet test USDC through x402.
The ESP32-C6 samples GPIO9 and signs a request-bound contact answer with P-256.
The buyer checks its pinned key, signature, request, samples, and ten-second age policy.

BOOT is the physical contact stand-in. Released produces OPEN. Held produces CLOSED.
The demo policy derives DISPATCH or WAIT. It controls no equipment.
Both honest states fulfill the observation. No escrow or payment-after-acceptance flow exists.

The October 11 transfer and original receipt are [public recorded evidence](evidence/device-signed-purchase-20261011.json).
The [verification record](VERIFICATION.md) distinguishes current and historical checks.
