Optional door-switch parts and acceptance tests

Know the limits

Answer the hard questions directly.

Is this just another dashboard?

The working buyer creates a job, settles a payment, and receives a new device-signed measurement. The screen exposes that mechanism. Film the physical input and transaction to make the mechanism visible.

Why does anyone pay?

A check needs to change a real decision or avoid a documented cost. We have not validated that buyer yet. The test price demonstrates payment behavior, not a business model.

Does a signature prove the device tells the truth?

No. It authenticates the provisioned key's claim and binds the claim to the request. Installation, calibration, truthful sensing, and protected key storage need separate controls.

What does peaq contribute today?

The buyer route checks a registered key before payment. The explicit official Storage route commits the completed job hash. The October 12 job passed a real identity-gated purchase and finalized Storage readback.

The published EventRegistry binds a different identity system. The Storage route records peaq activity without claiming an EventRegistry event or Machine Credit Rating. Inspect the deployment diagnostic.

Is this ready for Colosseum?

It is a credible technical prototype for an honest submission. Business viability and traction remain unproven. Real peaq identity and work evidence now support the sponsor entry. A prize is never guaranteed.

What can this become?

A common interface for independent devices to fulfill paid inspection jobs. First validate one recurring buyer need, then a second operator and integration reuse. A network remains a hypothesis.
