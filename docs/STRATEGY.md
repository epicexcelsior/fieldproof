[The story](competition/STORY.md) owns the product explanation and buyer hypothesis.
[The pilot questions](competition/PILOT.md) define the commercial test.

## Product risks and decisions

Validate one recurring buyer decision before building a provider network.
Measure its existing cost, required freshness, installation effort, and willingness to pay.
Production authorization, protected keys, clock assurance, and physical installation trust remain open.
A signature authenticates a device's claim. It does not establish truthful sensing.
